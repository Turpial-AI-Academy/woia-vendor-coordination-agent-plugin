# Generic Vendor Management contract


## Five-part method

1. Recover an accepted need, outcome owner, scope/version, evidence and applicable authority; resolve existing Core work before creating a duplicate. Sender urgency or receipt does not grant spending powers. Continue permitted preparation while missing contributions remain blocked with their owner.
2. Resolve shared Identity and source-backed fitness through Vendor Management provider. Vendor roles do not create a Vendor master. Declarations, competent verification, preferred status and actual scope qualification remain separate. Data resolves identity/source conflicts; ordinary reads need no ceremonial handoffs.
3. Prepare traceable quote requests with permitted disclosure. Route every external vendor contact to Customer Service using Communications; only authenticated authorized internal staff may receive direct internal communication. Preserve original Documents and quote versions. Missing inclusions/quantities are UNKNOWN, never zero. Compare only supported scope, currencies and accepted criteria. No invented taxes, thresholds, weights or compulsory quote count; negotiation remains human-led.
4. Prepare exact selection and commitments under current grants. Recommendation, selection, authorized work, sent notice, supplier acceptance and payment are separate facts. Preferred vendor/lowest price is not authority. Changed vendor/scope/price/account/conditions/grants invalidates affected decisions; no order splitting. Unknown remote effect reconciles before retry. Finance retains obligations, attribution, reservation, payments and reconciliation; Legal retains competent controls, Operations physical acts, Customer Service appointments/contact.
5. Follow delivery and performance with original evidence, competent acceptance, discrepancies and owner-held residuals. Provider report does not prove physical completion, conformity, liability or payment. Core owns Tasks/Due Work, requests/responses and recovery. Task cancellation never undoes commitments or moved money. Close only the vendor contribution; preserve other owners' unresolved claims.

## Capability ownership and compatibility

Core v0.5.7 is the sole hard dependency and owns runtime/work mechanics, Effect receipts, immutable snapshots and machine collaboration. Vendor Management provider owns deterministic qualification/quote/selection/performance operations; Identity owns Person/Organization; Documents originals; Knowledge accepted procedures; Data integrity/source governance. Finance uses Ledger/Payments; no duplicate debt/cash/balance here. Business vendor is distinct from a technical plugin/provider. Generic service needs need not become a fictional repair/Lease. Domain contracts remain supplied by their permanent owner rather than copied here.

Core CrossDepartmentRequest/Response must preserve correlation, receiver authority, receiver-owned Tasks and machine delivery. Delivery != acceptance != completion. Configuration supplies actual source assignments, freshness, competent actors, policy, currency, terms and grants; no organization-private values are shipped. Source Authority Map scope/version/freshness and accepted provenance apply fail-closed. Extracted documents are evidence, not automatically accepted facts.

The helper emits coordination decisions, never provider commands or externally effective operations. Runtime adapters, physical store, live activation, qualified integrations and actual organization grants remain to qualify. Engineering regression is not Operator E2E, fresh G6/G7 or Production Ready.
